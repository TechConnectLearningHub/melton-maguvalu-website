About Melton Maguvalu

Rooted in culture. United in sisterhood.

We are an independent, women-led not-for-profit bringing women together through culture, connection, empowerment and community support across Melbourne.

Discover our story Join the community
Our story

From one conversation to a growing community.

Melton Maguvalu began as a small WhatsApp group created to help women connect, share and support one another.

That simple space grew because women needed more than updates and introductions. They needed belonging. They needed a place to be heard without judgement, to celebrate culture with pride, and to build meaningful relationships beyond everyday responsibilities.

Today, Melton Maguvalu is a thriving sisterhood shaped by the women within it. Through networking, cultural events, wellbeing programs, community initiatives, workshops and support for women-led businesses, we create opportunities for women to connect, grow and lead.

“We are more than a community group. We are a safe space, a support system, a sisterhood and a movement.”
