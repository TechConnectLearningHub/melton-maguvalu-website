A festival with purpose

Flowers become a language of belonging.

Bathukamma brings generations together through floral artistry, music, dance and shared tradition. For Melton Maguvalu, the celebration is also a platform for women’s leadership, cultural pride, children’s participation and local collaboration.

Why it matters

One celebration.
Many layers of impact.

Bathukamma creates a shared space where culture is preserved, women lead, children participate and businesses contribute to a stronger local community.
